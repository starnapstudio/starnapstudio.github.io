Our promise

  • No ads, ever. No banners, no videos, nothing to watch for a reward.
  • Never pay-to-win. Everything that affects how you play is free for everyone. If we ever offer something to buy, it will be a small, cheap, purely cosmetic extra, there only for players who'd like to support the studio.
  • No pressure. No loot boxes, no countdown offers and no pop-ups asking you to pay.
  • No nagging notifications. If they ever come, it will be because players asked, and they'll be off unless you turn them on.
  • Your data stays yours. No tracking, no analytics, no account. Your progress lives on your device.

Support

Found a bug, got stuck, or just want to say hello? Write to starnapstudio@gmail.com. It helps to mention your phone model and what you were doing when it happened. We read every message.

Moving your progress to a new phone

Open Settings → Export save in the game and keep the code it shows somewhere safe. On the new phone, use Settings → Import save and paste it in.

Deleting your data

Orbital Genesis Protocol keeps your progress only on your device, and Starnap Studio never receives it. To delete it, use Settings → Reset all progress in the game, or simply uninstall the app. There is no account and nothing stored on any server, so there is nothing else to delete.
