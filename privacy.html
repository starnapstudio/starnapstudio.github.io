Privacy Policy

Orbital Genesis Protocol

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Orbital Genesis Protocol ("the game") is made by Starnap Studio. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

The game does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What the game stores

  • Your progress (credits, upgrades, nodes, your planet, settings and so on) is saved on your device, inside the app's own storage.
  • Automatic backups of your progress (the three most recent) are kept in the same place, so you can roll back from Settings.
  • Android backup: if you have turned on Google's device backup, Android may include the game's saved progress in your personal device backup. This is handled by Android under your Google account settings; the game itself sends nothing.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or ads. The game contains no purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No internet access: the app does not request the internet permission.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for the small haptic taps during play. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Exporting your save

If you use Export save, the game shows your progress as a text code. Where you keep that code is up to you; the game does not send it anywhere.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all progress in the game, or uninstall the app. Starnap Studio holds no copy of your data, so nothing else needs to be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.
